# ValFlix Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 19 November 2026*

ValFlix is a free app for Android TV, phones and tablets for browsing movies and TV shows and playing them through services you choose to link. It is made by adamsbuilds. This page explains what data ValFlix handles.

## No ValFlix servers, no tracking

ValFlix has no servers of its own. It does not collect analytics, does not show ads, and does not sell, share or upload your data to its developer. Everything ValFlix stores stays on your device.

## What is stored on your device

- Your settings (for example, preferred audio and subtitle languages and source filters).
- Sign-in tokens for the accounts you link (Trakt, Simkl, MDBList and debrid services), and any API keys you enter.
- Resume positions for things you have started watching, used for "Continue Watching".

Uninstalling ValFlix, or clearing its data, deletes all of this.

## Services ValFlix talks to

ValFlix sends requests directly from your device to these services, only to provide the features you use:

- **TMDb** – titles, artwork and details.
- **Trakt** (only if you link it) – reads your watchlist and recommendations to show them in the app, adds titles to your watchlist when you ask it to, and tells Trakt what you finished watching so it is marked as watched.
- **Simkl and MDBList** (only if you link them) – similar watchlist and list features.
- **Debrid services and source add-ons** you link or add – to find and play streams.
- **YouTube** – to play trailers.

Each service handles your data under its own privacy policy, for example [Trakt's privacy policy](https://trakt.tv/privacy).

## Linking and unlinking accounts

Accounts are linked with a code you approve on the service's own website; ValFlix never sees your passwords. You can unlink any account in ValFlix under Settings › Accounts or Settings › Debrid. For Trakt you can also revoke ValFlix's access at any time from your Trakt account's connected apps page.

## Phone setup page

If you use "Set up from your phone", ValFlix briefly runs a small web page on your home network so you can paste keys from your phone. It only runs while the QR code is on screen, uses a random address, and is not reachable from the internet.

## Trakt

**ValFlix uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.**

## Children

ValFlix is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
