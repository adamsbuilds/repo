# About ValFlix

> **ValFlix uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.**

ValFlix is a movie and TV show app for Android TV, phones and tablets, made by adamsbuilds. Trakt is optional: link it with a code to see your watchlist and recommendations and to have what you finish marked as watched. The same notice is shown inside the app under Settings › Accounts.
