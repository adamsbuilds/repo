# ValFlix

A movie and TV show app for Android TV, phones and tablets, by adamsbuilds.

ValFlix lets you browse trending, popular and top-rated movies and shows with artwork and details from TMDb, and play them through the services you choose to link.

## Trakt in ValFlix

- Link your Trakt account with a code: ValFlix shows a code and QR code, you approve it on trakt.tv.
- Your Trakt watchlist and recommendations appear as rows on the home screen.
- Add movies and shows to your Trakt watchlist from their details page.
- Movies and episodes you finish are marked as watched on Trakt.
- Unlink any time in Settings › Accounts, or revoke access from your Trakt account.

## Other features

- Simkl and MDBList watchlists and lists (optional).
- Linking of debrid services for playback, a built-in player with audio and subtitle language preferences, and in-app trailers.
- Designed for TV remotes first, with a phone layout.

## Privacy

ValFlix has no servers and collects no data; sign-ins and settings stay on your device. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
